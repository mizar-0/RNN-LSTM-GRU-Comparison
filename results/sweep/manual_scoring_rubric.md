# Blinded scoring rubric

Score each dimension from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent).

- **Coherence:** grammatical, readable progression with an intelligible overall meaning.
- **Contextual consistency:** remains related to the supplied prompt and does not introduce contradictory characters, places, or events.

Score poems without seeing model or temperature. Do not open `blinded_manual_scoring_key.csv` until scoring is complete. Generated text may contain crude user-submitted-limerick-style language.
