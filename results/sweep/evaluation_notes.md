# Sweep evaluation

Rhyme score is the mean of the A-A, B-B, A-final, and second-A-final rhyme checks, evaluated only for exactly five-line poems whose five line-ending words are all covered by CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. Unterminated poems are retained and count as failures for termination and five-line form. Bootstrap intervals are 95% percentile intervals with 2,000 resamples.
